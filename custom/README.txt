Put your own images here (PNG, JPG or GIF), and sound files (MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A, AAC or FLAC).
In the control panel, tap Custom… (the Custom background on the Animated L3s tab, or the Custom Fullscreen L3) to pick which image to use.
Until you pick one, the first by file name is used.
The Custom… stinger (the last one under Stingers) has its own pick: a PNG with a clear background, like a logo, works best.
The Sound Pad's Custom pad plays a sound file from here: tap the pad (or the folder in its corner) to pick it.
