Saved shows go here, one .json file each, named by number and title (like "001 - Morning Show.json").
In the control panel, tap the show name in the top bar to save the show or open another one.
A show keeps everything the panel is set to: saved L3s and overlays, the rundown, the L3 Builder, Simple mode
and its theme, the picks on each tab, light or dark mode, the timer, the projector, show times and more.
AKTA settings aren't in it: they stay with each computer.
Custom images aren't in it either: copy the custom folder too when you move a show to another computer.
Number 999 is restricted to engineering.
