Put the pictures (PNG, JPG, GIF or WebP) and videos (MP4 or WebM) you want to show on screen here.
In the control panel, on the Fullscreen Overlays tab, open Overlays and tap Content… (on a TV) or Content Full… (full screen) to pick one.
A picture stays up 15 seconds. A video plays all the way through, once, with its sound (turn on "Control audio via OBS" on the FX Display browser source).
