Put the pictures (PNG, JPG, GIF or WebP) and videos (MP4 or WebM) you want to show on screen here.
In the control panel, on the Fullscreen Overlays tab, open Content and tap Content… (on a TV) or Content Full… (filling the source) to pick one.
Content shows on its own Browser Source in OBS: fx_wizard.html#content (below fx_wizard.html#display). It stays up while lower thirds and overlays come and go.
A picture stays up 15 seconds. A video plays all the way through, once, with its sound (turn on "Control audio via OBS" on the FX Content browser source).
